# 00 — Product Brief

## Working name

**GymOS**

## Product thesis

Most gyms do not primarily suffer from a lack of dashboards. They lose revenue and control through missed renewals, unworked leads, inconsistent discounts, unclear payments, poor staff follow-up, and fragmented member records.

GymOS is a **revenue and operations operating system for gyms**. Its central promise is:

> Never lose a membership renewal, sales lead, payment, or staff follow-up again.

## Initial market

- Jordan first.
- Arabic/English and RTL-ready from the start.
- Designed to expand into GCC/MENA markets through configuration and country-specific integrations.
- Initial ideal customer: one to three branches, roughly 300–2,000 members, dedicated reception, meaningful sales/renewal activity, and fragmented current processes.

## Primary users

### Owner

Needs revenue visibility, branch comparison, leakage alerts, suspicious-action visibility, and concise daily summaries.

### General manager / branch manager

Needs operational control, staff accountability, task queues, approvals, reconciliation, and performance reporting.

### Sales representative

Needs a prioritized pipeline, member history, follow-up tasks, call outcomes, offers, and conversion attribution.

### Receptionist

Needs very fast member lookup, check-in, membership validation, payment collection, receipt issuance, and clear warnings.

### Trainer

Future or limited MVP role. Needs assigned clients, session balances, schedule, and notes. Do not turn this into an independent-trainer marketplace in the MVP.

## Core lifecycle

```text
Lead
  → contact attempt
  → trial / visit
  → offer
  → membership sale
  → payment
  → check-ins and engagement
  → renewal
  → reactivation if expired
```

All lifecycle events should appear in one **Member 360 timeline**.

## MVP modules

1. Organization, branches, users, roles, and permissions.
2. Members and member profiles.
3. Membership plans, subscriptions, freezes, extensions, and balances.
4. Leads, sales pipeline, calls, tasks, offers, and renewals.
5. Reception desk and check-in.
6. Payments, receipts, discounts, refunds/voids, and daily cash reconciliation.
7. Basic automations and message/task triggers.
8. Owner and manager dashboards.
9. Audit log and approval flows for sensitive actions.
10. CSV import/export for practical onboarding.

## Product differentiation

- Jordan/MENA-native workflows rather than a translated Western product.
- WhatsApp-oriented outreach architecture.
- Cash and mixed-payment reconciliation.
- Strong renewal and lead follow-up queues.
- Explicit staff accountability and immutable audit trails.
- Fast reception workflow.
- Arabic/RTL readiness.
- Owner-facing daily operational brief.
- AI may assist later, but the system must work completely without AI.

## Product boundaries

### In the MVP

- B2B web application.
- Responsive enough for tablets and mobile browsers.
- Multi-branch gym organizations.
- Operational and financial workflows listed above.

### Not in the MVP

- Public multi-gym discovery marketplace.
- Cross-gym sharing of private member history.
- Independent personal-trainer marketplace.
- Native iOS/Android applications.
- Full workout-programming platform.
- Payroll, full accounting, or ERP replacement.
- Complex biometric storage.
- AI call recording/transcription.

## Success metrics

The product should make these measurable:

- Leads contacted within target time.
- Trial-to-membership conversion.
- Expiring members contacted.
- Renewal rate.
- Reactivation revenue.
- Unresolved payment balances.
- Daily cash variance.
- Discount/refund volume by employee.
- Check-in and inactivity trends.
- Sales revenue and conversion by representative.

The MVP is commercially validated when a real gym can operate its daily reception, sales follow-up, memberships, and reconciliation through it without parallel spreadsheets for those workflows.

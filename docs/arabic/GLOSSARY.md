# Arabic terminology

The locked [STANDARD.md](STANDARD.md), [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), and [approved v1 export](approved-decisions.v1.json) supersede the earlier foundation glossary. Catalog `2026-09-30-v1`, revision `607`, contains 247 jointly agreed decisions. Run `python3 docs/arabic/verify-lock.py` to verify the preserved evidence.

Use the approved context, not a global word substitution. Purchased memberships are اشتراك; member navigation is الأعضاء while the search and timeline use المشترك. The cash payment method is كاش; drawer accounting retains the agreed الصندوق phrases. A receipt is وصل دفع and its email subject is إيصال دفعتك. The marketing promise is exactly كل تفاصيل ناديك و مشتركينه في مكان واحد.

Arabic uses Latin digits, Gregorian dates with Jordanian month names, 12-hour time and three decimal places for JOD (`25.000 د.أ`). General voice is clear, mostly formal Modern Standard Arabic. Specific agreed wording takes precedence.

The [historical foundation glossary](history/foundation-glossary-2026-10-01.md) is retained for provenance only. Its claim that approvals were missing and its old currency/drawer terminology are superseded. Implementation coverage is tracked separately and remains incomplete until every package is verified.
